A sudden ⊹ flood of light

A few rays seep into you before you realize you are open
The rest reflect off you, shattering into icy iridescent shards

Hands made of these fractures coax the wet sand of your body into place
Sometimes, they rework the same spot, arguing with each other:

“This should be blurred like the ocean’s waves at midnight”
“No, let’s leave it dimpled like the moon”

Their touch leaves glowing traces along your hills and valleys
Light-glossed sand-skin no longer remembers its previous shape

Breathe in
A fire starts beneath you, making your sand a smooth solid

Breathe out
Your grains cool, glass coats you

A hand caresses the flame
Smoke tickles your feet
