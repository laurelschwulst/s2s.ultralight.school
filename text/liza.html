1) What of the few feeewho whoee? Do-eeee, dee-o? A friend saw a hummingbird here last week. She says to pinch a flower between thumb and finger. Hold still and listen for the buzzing crisp. Pause. 2) Tehehe, wetehe. Tweeetttee-tee. You called me bird when I was young, remember? Or, maybe it was the opposite. 3) Cheep cheep cheep! Soft whizz, hummmmmm. Turn one direction and there’s nothing. Look up and find the highest point a bird might reach as a small dot. Draw a circle with your spine. 4) Uueeyyee, oo oo. Twindle chweep chew chew, chatter. Interrupts, [coughs in the distance]. Repeat. 5) Whrrrmmmm, bum bum bum bum. H tells me how to sing to the worms. She jumps into the ground in a swan dive. The earth splits open expectingly. 6) Chee chee chee chee chee chee on on on and on and... Open your arms and spin spin spin until you begin to fly. Interrupts, [rooowwfff, as in, a warning]. 7) Woo tee tee twee tee, two two two. A wind chime responds to the sparse breeze, refracting winged gradients. A stray bell. 8) Home home, hummmmmm. H recalls her first day as a worm charmer. I recall my last day as a bird.
